## Nightime Lights Workshop

Split into Groups of 3

1. Create a new QMD called nighttime_lights.qmd
2. Folow the steps outline [here](https://worldbank.github.io/blackmarbler/index.html) until you reach "Workflow to update data"
3. Adapt the analysis of Ghana for Hawaii and recreate visualizations
4. Pick a storm, Draft a strategy to measure Hawaii power outages during that storm
  - Think of baseline dates, event dates, cloud cover, news reported data, visualization techniques, etc
  - Write the strategy at the bottom of your r script
5. Read this [paper](https://github.com/NSF-ALL-SPICE-Alliance/DS422-Geocomputation/blob/main/papers/Monitoring_global_power_outages.pdf)
